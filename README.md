# 🚴‍♂️ Divvy Bike Share Case Study (Google Data Analytics)

## 📌 Project Overview
This project is part of the **Google Data Analytics Professional Certificate** capstone project. 
The objective is to analyze historical bike-share data from Divvy (Chicago) to understand how **Annual Members** and **Casual Riders** use bikes differently. The ultimate business goal is to derive data-backed strategies to convert casual riders into annual members.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Methodology
* **Tool Used:** Microsoft Excel
* **Data Cleaning & Processing:** Created `ride_length` and `day_of_week` columns, cleaned missing/duplicate data, and filtered out invalid trip durations.
* **Data Analysis:** Aggregated data using **Pivot Tables** to calculate total trip counts and average ride durations.
* **Data Visualization:** Built **Pivot Charts** to highlight key user behavioral patterns.

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Findings

### 1. Ride Duration Difference
* **Casual Riders** ride significantly longer per trip compared to members.
* **Annual Members** take shorter, more frequent trips (likely commuting to work or school).

### 2. Usage by Day of the Week
* **Casual Riders:** Usage peaks during **Weekends (Saturday & Sunday)**, indicating leisure and recreational use.
* **Annual Members:** Usage is higher on **Weekdays (Monday–Friday)**, confirming routine commuting patterns.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
Based on the data insights, here are three marketing strategies to convert casual riders into annual members:

1. **Weekend / Seasonal Membership Plans:**
   * Since casual riders primarily ride on weekends, introduce a **Weekend Pass** or **Summer Membership** to attract them without requiring a full 1-year commitment upfront.
2. **Targeted Promotions for Long Trips:**
   * Offer discounts or rewards to casual riders who ride longer than 30 minutes, showing them how much money they would save with an Annual Membership.
3. **Digital & On-Site Marketing:**
   * Run targeted digital ad campaigns near popular leisure spots, parks, and tourist locations on weekends.
